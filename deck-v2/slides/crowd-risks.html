Part 2 · Unlloo crowd loans

The honest risks, for both sides

No collateral

An unpaid loan is a loss for its lenders. No pool, no insurance.

No repayment history yet

Unlloo has no real-money track record, and repaying does not raise the score yet.

The score is information

The contract does not check it. Any wallet can ask for a loan.

An admin key

One wallet can pause new loans and upgrade after a 3-day timelock. Repayments and claims cannot be paused.

Locked offers

Funds can stay locked until the funding deadline plus the 7-day selection window.

Stablecoin freeze

USDC or USDT can be frozen by the issuer. DAI has no freeze.

Loans have no collateral, so lending can lose money: read the risks and admin powers before you fund.

0:53 – Before the feedback round, the risks, in plain words. This is my own project, so be the first to say what can go wrong. Read the six cards, one sentence each. – No collateral: the lenders on a loan lose what they lent if it is not repaid. There is no insurance fund and no shared pool, so a default stays with that loan’s lenders. – No repayment history yet: Unlloo has no real-money repayment record, and repaying an Unlloo loan does not raise the score yet. – The score is information, not a gate: the contract does not check it. – An admin key: a single wallet can pause new loans, offers and acceptances (not repayments, claims or refunds), cancel requests still in funding, and upgrade contract logic after a public 3-day timelock. – Locked offers: withdrawals are not automatic. If offers reach 80%, a lender’s money can stay locked until the funding deadline plus the 7-day selection window. – Stablecoin freeze: USDC and USDT can be blocked by their issuers, and a block on the contract affects every user of it. DAI has no blocklist. Spreading loans across tokens helps, but DAI fell with USDC in March 2023. Also say: no KYC at the contract level, new wallets get little lender interest, and Unlloo is young. Start small on both sides.
