Rate: Morpho app, WBTC/USDC on Ethereum, 19 Nov 2026. Midnight launch dates: The Block (Base), The Defiant (Ethereum).

0:24 – Morpho, the second protocol to know. Over $16B in deposits; builders include Coinbase and Société Générale. Morpho markets (Morpho Blue): each market is one collateral + one loan asset with fixed parameters. Nothing is re-lent, so a bad asset in another market can’t hurt you. – Dollar markets on 19 Nov 2026: WBTC against USDC at 86% LLTV costs about 6.2% on Ethereum, with about $109M borrowed and $11M free to borrow. cbBTC against USDC on Base costs about 5.3%. – That is not cheaper than Aave right now. The reason to use Morpho is isolation and the higher LLTV, not the rate. – Catch: there is no close factor. A liquidator can repay 100% of your debt at once, so keep a higher health factor here than on Aave. Morpho Midnight: fixed-rate, fixed-term loans built into the protocol. Launched on Base on 21 July 2026 and on Ethereum on 8 September 2026 (USDC against WBTC or cbBTC). Good when you want to know your cost for the whole period. Tokenping (tokenping.xyz) sends reminders before Midnight loans mature. Quick demo if time allows: open the Morpho app and show a WBTC market’s LLTV, rate and liquidity.
