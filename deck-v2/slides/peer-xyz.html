0:32 – peer.xyz (built on the ZKP2P protocol). The cleanest way I know to turn borrowed USDC into dollars on your own account without KYC. As the seller of USDC (that’s you when cashing out): 1. You deposit USDC into the escrow smart contract, choose which payment apps and currencies you accept, and set your rate. 2. A buyer picks your offer and pays you dollars on Revolut, Wise, Venmo and so on. 3. The buyer generates a cryptographic proof of that payment (zkTLS / TEE). Only amount, recipient and timestamp are revealed. 4. The contract verifies the proof and releases the USDC to the buyer. You already have the dollars. Why it’s safe-ish: neither side has to trust the other or a company – the USDC sits in a contract until the payment is proven. “Built on proofs, not promises.” Fees: buyers pay a taker fee of up to 0.95%; bridging to another chain up to 0.5%. Sellers keep their spread. Caution: it’s still a young (beta) protocol. Start small. And remember your bank still sees incoming payments from strangers – keep amounts reasonable.
