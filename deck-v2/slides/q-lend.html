0:41 – The first question of Part 2, about 2 minutes. Ask it before you explain anything about Unlloo, so the answers are not coloured by the pitch. Read the question: would you lend $500 to a wallet you have never seen, with no collateral and no contract? Then go through the five options and count hands for each, out loud. People may raise a hand more than once. Write the counts down (or ask someone to): this is your first piece of feedback, and you will compare it with what people say at the end. Follow up with one or two people: – To someone who chose “Nothing helps”: what would have to be true for you to change your mind? – To someone who chose a score: what would the score have to be built from? – To someone who chose a friend: would you vouch for a friend with your own money? Don’t defend or explain yet. The next slide is the honest answer to what can replace collateral.
