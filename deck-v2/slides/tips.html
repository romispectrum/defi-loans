Six habits that keep you alive

01

Never press Max

Max borrow runs to HF 1.0. One wick and you’re liquidated.

02

Stress-test −50%

Start at HF 2.4 or more: still 1.2 if BTC halves.

03

Two alert channels

Signal plus email on tokenping.xyz. One app will fail you one day.

04

Keep a reserve

Stablecoins on the same chain, ready to repay in one click.

05

Rehearse with $10

Practise adding collateral and repaying before 3 a.m. forces you to.

06

Pay the interest

Debt grows every second. HF sinks even when BTC doesn’t.

0:36 – Rapid fire, about 20 seconds per card. These are the habits I see separating people who use loans for years from people who get liquidated once and quit. 1. Never press Max. On Aave v4 borrow power goes to HF 1.0. 2. Stress test: HF halves when BTC halves. If you want to survive −50% and still sit at 1.2, start at 2.4. (My old rule: always be ready for a 50% drop.) 3. Two alert channels – Signal and email on Tokenping (tokenping.xyz), for example. Any single app can be down at the wrong moment. 4. Keep a stablecoin reserve on the same chain as the loan. Bridging during a crash is slow and expensive. 5. Rehearse: do one add-collateral and one partial repay with $10 so your hands know the flow. 6. Pay accrued interest from time to time. It is added to the debt continuously.
