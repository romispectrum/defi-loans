0:17 – Everything we use in the next ten minutes. Six tools. Rabby (rabby.io): an open-source wallet that simulates every transaction before you sign and shows your Aave position – health factor, liquidation price. Download only from rabby.io; fake copies exist. From my old workshop: Rabby is no longer available for Firefox, so use Brave or Chrome. Hardware wallet: keep the signing keys off the laptop for anything bigger than pocket money. Rabby connects to Trezor, Ledger, BitBox and others. Brave (brave.com) plus a VPN: your wallet talks to RPC servers that see your IP. A VPN plus a privacy browser keeps the address-to-person link weaker. A cheap chain: Arbitrum, Base or Avalanche. The transaction that saves you during a crash must cost cents, not $50 of mainnet gas. DeFiLlama (defillama.com): the neutral dashboard to compare borrow rates across protocols and chains before you pick one. Tokenping (tokenping.xyz): my project. Health factor alerts on Signal, Telegram and email. More on it later, when we talk about staying alive.
