0:47 – How Unlloo works today: uncollateralized crowd loans on Ethereum mainnet. Lenders decide on the borrower’s reputation, because there is no collateral. Borrower side: 1. Publish a request: amount (1 to 100,000 USDC), maximum APR (up to 50%), funding deadline, repayment window. Lenders see your reputation score. 2. Lenders make offers – any amount, each at their own rate from 0% up to your cap. Up to 200 offers per loan, each locked in the contract when submitted. 3. If offers reach at least 80% of the request, you get a 7-day window to pick which offers to accept. No automatic matching. Under 80%, the request expires and lenders withdraw in full. 4. Funds arrive in USDC, USDT or DAI on Ethereum mainnet. Nothing of yours is locked, seized or liquidated. 5. Repay: simple interest accrues per second at each lender’s rate; repaying early has no penalty. Interest first, then principal. Cost: a whole loan was about $0.36 in gas for the borrower on 28 October 2026 (0.2 gwei). The protocol takes 10% of interest (capped at 25%), never principal; fee changes go through a 3-day timelock. The score (0–1000): 60% from Human Passport, Webacy, Ethos, Talent Protocol, growthepie and Zeru zPass, 40% from Unlloo’s own wallet-history score over 48 months on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Avalanche and Optimism. Wallets under 12 months get nothing from the history part. Unlloo repayment history isn’t in the score yet, and the contract doesn’t check the score – lenders do. Lender side: you choose who to fund and at what rate. Be honest about the risk: an unrepaid loan is a loss – no collateral, pool or insurance. Fund only what you could lose on that one loan. Tip: age your main wallet and collect identity stamps (Human Passport, Ethos vouches, Talent Protocol). Ask a friend for the first offer. Live: open unlloo.com and look up your own score.
