0:59 – The cheat sheet for the whole hour. Leave it up while people take photos and ask questions. Walk it top to bottom: – Need cash on a bank account with a contract and you’re fine with KYC? Firefish. – Privacy, flexibility, no end date? Aave: V3 for maturity and liquidity, v4 if you want each position’s risk kept in its own spoke. – One collateral against one loan asset, nothing shared with other markets? A Morpho market. – You want certainty about cost and date? Morpho Midnight or PWN. – No collateral at all? Unlloo, if your onchain reputation is good enough. – Just want leverage on BTC? A loan loop works, but perps (small, isolated margin, with a stop-loss) are usually cleaner. Above 10× leverage is basically a donation to the exchange. – Want the other side? Supply stablecoins, or lend on Unlloo at your own rate. On price: the DeFi routes all cost 4–6.5% in dollars today, so choose on risk and convenience first.
