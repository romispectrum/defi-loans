0:16 – Aave and Morpho live on Ethereum-style chains, so native BTC can’t go in directly. You need a token that represents BTC. Today’s examples use WBTC. The question to ask about every wrapper: who holds the real BTC, and can they freeze or refuse to redeem it? – WBTC: BitGo custody. Oldest, longest track record, the deepest liquidity on Aave. – tBTC (Threshold Network): a rotating group of signers, no single custodian. Minting is free, redeeming costs 0.2%. Lupták’s preferred option for decentralization. – cbBTC: Coinbase. Very common on Base and Morpho. – BTC.b: Lombard’s custodial wrapper on Avalanche – what you use on Aave v4 Avalanche. – cirBTC: Circle’s BTC on Arc, its permissioned chain. How to get one: swap on a non-KYC instant exchanger such as trocador.app or ff.io (small risk of a freeze on flagged coins), mint tBTC yourself, or P2P. Tip: a wrapper adds one more party you trust. With a large stack, don’t put everything in one wrapper. Links: https://trocador.app · https://ff.io
