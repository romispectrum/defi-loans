# DeFi Loans Workshop deck, V3

Source files of the V3 slide deck published at https://claude.ai/artifact/UDeBMkiQRWci3UsD6rbJ5E
(artifact version 3, saved 2026-11-19). V2 is in `../deck-v2/`, V1 in `../deck/`.

Event name: "DeFi loans from collateralized to uncollateralized". 39 slides, 60 minutes, all amounts and rates in USD.

- **Part 1, 40 minutes: collateralized loans.** Unchanged from V2.
- **Part 2, 20 minutes: uncollateralized loans.** A discussion to collect feedback on Unlloo crowd loans.

What V3 changes in Part 2:

- The opening question asks about $1,000 (`q-lend`).
- "Why we build Unlloo" (`unlloo-story`) is the gap between a bank ($10,000 in two clicks, no collateral) and crypto (always collateral), with no hackathon story.
- New `crowd-loan` slide: many lenders, one borrower.
- The reputation score is toned down: no score card, no score in the exercise or the feedback questions, no column of score providers on the links slide.
- `no-collateral` moved after the worked example.

Files: `deck.json` is the index; `slides/<id>.html` is one slide per file, with speaker notes in the `<aside>` (they start with the minute the slide begins).

Open placeholders on the last slide: Signal group link or QR, Lightning address.
