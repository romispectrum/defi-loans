Find your liquidation price

The setup

Collateral: 1 WBTC at $60,000

Threshold: 78%

You borrow: $20,000

  1. What is your health factor?
  2. At what BTC price are you liquidated?
  3. How far can BTC fall before that?

Answers

HF = 2.34

60,000 × 0.78 ÷ 20,000

Liquidated at $25,641

20,000 ÷ (1 × 0.78)

BTC can fall 57%

Shortcut: drop to liquidation = 1 − 1 ÷ HF
