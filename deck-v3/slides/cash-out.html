0:31 – You borrowed stablecoins. Rent, food and flights are paid from a bank account or a card. Three ways across: P2P, cards, exchanges. – peer.xyz (next slide): no KYC, the dollars land on the account you already use. – ether.fi Cash: a non-custodial Visa card on Scroll – you keep the keys in your own Safe. It spends straight from USDC or USDT. KYC. – Jeton: a bank account with free stablecoin top-ups (ERC20, TRC20, Solana) and next-day transfers. KYC. – Uglycash: an eUSD-based account with US account and routing numbers and a Visa debit card. KYC. – Freedomia: a prepaid Visa without KYC, topped up with BTC (also Lightning), USDT, USDC or ETH. It is a card, not a bank account. – Kraken: sell USDC for dollars and withdraw to your bank – the boring, well-documented route for larger amounts. Practical tip: for spending, any card works. For bank transfers, pick something with account details in your own name. Always test with $20 first.
