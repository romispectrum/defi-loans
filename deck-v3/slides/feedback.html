0:54 – The feedback round, 5 minutes. This is the point of Part 2: you are here to learn what the room thinks, not to convince it. How to run it: – Ask someone to take notes, or record the audio with the room’s permission. You will not remember the answers. – One question at a time. Take two or three answers, then move on. Don’t try to cover all six: pick the three that matter most to you today. – Don’t defend the product. When someone objects, ask “why?” or “what would change your mind?” and write it down. – Quiet room? Go back to the hands from the first question (“would you lend $1,000 to a stranger?”) and ask whether anyone changed their answer, and why. What I most want to learn from each question: 1. Borrow: real use cases, and whether anyone here would actually request a loan. 2. Trust: what counts most, a track record, an identity check, a friend who vouches, or part collateral. 3. Rate: the number people say. Compare it with the 8–15% range the first requests use. 4. Default: do people expect a public record, an identity reveal, a guarantor, a legal route? 5. Blockers: the admin key, no track record, locked funds, mainnet gas, stablecoin risk. 6. Missing: write down every feature request, even the odd ones. Close: thank the room and say what you will do with the feedback. Anyone who wants to try a first request or offer can find you after the session.
