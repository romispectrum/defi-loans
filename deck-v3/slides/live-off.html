0:34 – How people actually “live off a crypto loan” for years. 1. BTC goes up → your LTV drops automatically, HF rises. The position heals itself. 2. When LTV is low again, borrow a bit more against the same coins. Still no sale, still no taxable event at that moment. 3. Spend via card, bank transfer or peer.xyz. No BTC is sold. 4. Rates float: when something cheaper appears (another stablecoin, another chain, another protocol), move the debt. On Aave/Morpho there’s no maturity, so no repeated origination fee like Firefish. Two rules that keep it working: – In a bear market, deleverage early. Lupták’s target: survive a 70–80% drop. – Interest is added to your debt. After a few years of never paying anything, HF erodes even if BTC is flat. Pay accrued interest from time to time or hold a reserve. Worked example from the article: hold 2 BTC, wrap 1 as WBTC, borrow $20k of stablecoins (LTV ~33%, HF ~2.3), spend by card or bank transfer, alert at HF 2. Cost about $800–1,300 a year at today’s 4–6.5%.
