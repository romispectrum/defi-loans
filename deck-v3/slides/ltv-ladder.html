0:13 – The ladder. Same WBTC, different LTVs. At 25–30% LTV you survive a 62–68% crash without touching anything. At 50% you are one bad week away (−36%). At 60% a normal BTC correction liquidates you. History: BTC fell about 77% from Nov 2021 to Nov 2022, and about 84% in 2018. So “BTC never falls 60%” is not a plan. Recommendation from the Opportunist article and from my own practice: 25–35% LTV, health factor 2+ in calm markets, 2.5–3 when it gets volatile. Ask: “Which row are you in today, honestly?”
