0:40 – Part 2, and the format changes: from here on this is a discussion, not a lecture. Say so. Everything so far was over-collateralized: you lock more than you borrow. That works only for people who already have assets. The line, left to right: – Secured: Aave, Morpho, Firefish. More than 100% locked. – Peer-to-peer on fixed terms: PWN, Morpho Midnight. Still collateralized, but negotiated. – Credit lines: Maple, Wildcat, 3Jane. Little or no collateral, but for vetted institutions and businesses, underwritten off-chain, usually with KYC and a legal contract. – Crowd loans: Unlloo, my project. Many lenders fund one borrower and nothing is locked. This is what the next 20 minutes are about. – Flash loans: zero collateral, but borrowed and repaid inside one transaction, so they are a tool, not credit. Why it is hard: with no collateral there is nothing to liquidate, so the lender has to trust the borrower. Wallets are cheap to create, so that trust has to come from somewhere. Don’t ask the room yet: the question is the next slide.
