0:43 – Why we build Unlloo, in two minutes. No history lesson, just the gap. In a bank today you can borrow, say, $10,000 in about two clicks in the app. No collateral. The bank knows your income and your history, so it lends on trust and prices the risk. In crypto it is the opposite. Every loan we looked at in Part 1 needs collateral, and more than you borrow: to get $10,000 you first lock $15,000 to $20,000. You need money to borrow money. That is not how the real world borrows. It is leverage: very useful if you are a trader, or if you hold BTC and don’t want to sell. It is useless if you need to pay rent, bridge an invoice or start something. So the point: if crypto is going to be used by normal people for normal things, it needs loans without collateral. That is what makes lending useful, and not only a tool for a bigger trade. Unlloo is our attempt at it: crowd loans, where many people each lend a small part to one borrower. That is the next slide. Numbers behind the slide: DeFi lending is a market of more than $40B, with $1.50 to $2.00 locked for every $1 borrowed. Unsecured credit in traditional finance is more than $5T (Unlloo blog, May 2026). Discuss: what would you borrow for, if you didn’t need collateral? Collect two or three answers and write them down.
