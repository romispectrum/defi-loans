0:46 – How an Unlloo crowd loan works today, on Ethereum mainnet. Five steps, 20 seconds each. 1. Request: the borrower sets an amount (1 to 100,000 USDC), a maximum yearly rate (up to 50%), a funding deadline and a repayment window. 2. Offers: lenders each offer any amount at their own rate, from 0% up to the borrower’s cap. Up to 200 offers per loan, locked in the contract while funding runs. 3. Select: if offers reach at least 80% of the request, the borrower has 7 days to pick which offers to accept. Under 80% the request expires and lenders withdraw in full. 4. Borrow: the money arrives in USDC, USDT or DAI. Nothing of the borrower’s is locked, seized or liquidated. 5. Repay: simple interest per second at each lender’s own rate, interest first, then principal. Early repayment has no penalty. Cost: a whole loan was about $0.36 in gas for the borrower on 28 October 2026. Unlloo keeps 10% of the interest (capped at 25%), never principal. For lenders: you choose who to fund and at what rate, and you can look at the borrower’s wallet history first. Be honest about the risk: an unpaid loan is a loss. No collateral, no pool, no insurance. Fund only what you could lose on that one loan. Discuss: which of the five steps would stop you, as a borrower or as a lender? Note the step people name most.
