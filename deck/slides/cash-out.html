0:32 – You borrowed stablecoins. Rent is paid in euros. Three ways across the bridge: P2P, cards, exchanges. – peer.xyz (next slide): no KYC, euros land on the bank account you already use. – ether.fi Cash: non-custodial Visa on Scroll – you keep the keys in your own Safe. Pays directly from USDC/USDT/EURC, 0% FX on EUR and USD, also SEPA/IBAN. KYC. – Jeton: strong SEPA/IBAN, free stablecoin top-ups (ERC20, TRC20, Solana), next-day SEPA, FX roughly 0.2% + 0.5%. KYC. – Uglycash: eUSD-based account with EU IBAN, US account/routing and a Visa debit card. KYC. – Freedomia: prepaid Visa without KYC, top up with BTC (also Lightning), USDT, USDC or ETH. It’s a card, not a SEPA account. – Kraken: accepts EURC and pays out via SEPA – the boring, well-documented route for larger amounts. Practical tip: for spending, any card works. For bank transfers, pick something with an IBAN. For EURC without FX, ether.fi. Always test with $20 first.
