Borrower: how to get funded

Amount

100–500 USDC

Start small. The contract allows up to 100,000.

Rate cap

8–15% a year

Lenders bid under it. The contract allows 5–50%.

Funding window

7–14 days

Under a day, nobody sees it. The contract allows 1 hour to 90 days.

Repay in

30–90 days

Counted from the end of the 7-day selection window.

First offer

A friend

Ask one person who knows you before you publish. Strangers follow.

Your record

On time

Every outcome is public on-chain. Repay, then borrow more.

Source: Unlloo blog, how to request a loan lenders fund and how to borrow. Your own numbers will differ.

0:49 – The practical settings for a first crowd loan, from the Unlloo blog. These are suggestions, not rules; the contract allows far more. – Amount: 100 to 500 USDC. A request must reach 80% of its size before the loan starts, so a 5,000 USDC request needs 4,000 in offers before anything happens. – Rate cap: 8% to 15% a year. It is a ceiling: lenders bid under it, and the final rate can be lower. A 5% cap from an unknown wallet leaves lenders no room to price the risk. – Funding window: 7 to 14 days. In the blog’s example, offers arrived on days two to five. A window under a day gives lenders no time to see the request. – Repayment: 30 to 90 days, counted from the end of the 7-day selection window, not from when you post. Repaying early costs nothing extra. – Ask one person who knows you to make the first offer. Early offers draw in strangers. – Repay on time. Every outcome (on time, late, unpaid) is written to the chain and is what the next lender reads. Who it suits: freelancers paid in stablecoins, small online businesses, people who move between countries, friends and communities lending to each other. Not a fit for brand-new wallets or for anyone who needs local-currency cash today.
