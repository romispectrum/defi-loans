0:47 – One crowd loan from start to finish, with made-up numbers (say so). The point: the rate is not a formula, it is the average of what real lenders asked for. Request: 1,000 USDC, a rate cap of 15% a year, offers open for 7 days, repayment within 90 days. Three lenders: A offers 500 at 10%, B 300 at 12%, C 200 at 14%. Together that is 1,000 USDC, well above the 80% (800) the borrower needs, so the borrower can pick any combination. Accepting all three gives a blended rate of 11.4% a year. Interest per second on each lender’s own amount and rate, simple interest. Over 90 days: A 12.33, B 8.88, C 6.90, total 28.11 USDC. Unlloo keeps 10% of interest (2.81), never principal; lenders keep 25.30. The borrower repays 1,028.11 USDC and can repay early with no penalty. Cost: the Unlloo blog measured gas on Ethereum mainnet on 28 October 2026: about $0.36 for a borrower’s whole loan and $0.22 for each lender, at 0.2 gwei. At 20 gwei the same loan would cost about $34, so gas depends on the day. Compare how other protocols set rates: Aave and Morpho use a formula (a utilization curve), auctions clear at one rate, crowd loans use named offers. The blog’s own example: offers of 10%, 12% and 14% on a 1,000 USDC loan for 90 days blended to 11.8% and about 29.10 USDC of interest.
