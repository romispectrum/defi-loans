Flash loans: zero collateral

One transaction · all or nothing

Borrow 1,000,000 USDC

Use it: swap, refinance, liquidate

Repay 1,000,000 + a small fee

Not repaid? The whole transaction reverts, as if it never happened.

You may already use them

  • Swap collateral (WBTC → tBTC) without closing the loan
  • Repay debt with your collateral in one click
  • Move a loan to a cheaper protocol
  • Unwind a leveraged loop at once

The dark side

Attackers borrow firepower too: the bZx (2020) and Euler (2023) exploits ran on flash loans.

0:39 – The most extreme form of undercollateralized lending: zero collateral. And it’s safe for the lender. How: a smart contract lends you any amount the pool has, you use it, and you repay principal plus a small fee – all inside the same transaction. If the repayment is missing, the entire transaction reverts as if it never happened. The lender can’t lose; you just paid gas for a failed transaction. Aave and Morpho both offer flash loans. You don’t need to write code to benefit: “swap collateral”, “repay with collateral” and one-click refinancing or deleveraging tools are built on flash loans under the hood. Hack for the room: if you ever want to move from WBTC to tBTC collateral, or from Aave V3 to v4, you don’t need to repay first – a flash-loan-powered tool can move the position in one transaction. (Always simulate in Rabby first.) Dark side: many DeFi exploits used flash loans to borrow huge amounts for a few seconds – bZx in 2020, Euler in 2023. The flash loan isn’t the bug; it amplifies an existing bug.
