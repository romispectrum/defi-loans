02 · How loans work

Four numbers run your loan

33%

Loan-to-value (LTV)

Debt ÷ collateral value. The one number you control.

73%

Max LTV

The most the app lets you borrow. A limit, not a target.

78%

Liquidation threshold

Cross this LTV and liquidators sell part of your collateral.

2.34

Health factor

Your safety gauge. Below 1.0 you get liquidated.

HF = collateral × liquidation threshold ÷ debt

= $60,000 × 0.78 ÷ $20,000 = 2.34

Example values: WBTC on Aave V3 Ethereum (Max LTV 73%, liquidation threshold 78%), Oct 2026, per Cryptoactu. Parameters change by vote: Aave docs · parameters. How it works: Aave health factor, Morpho liquidation.

0:10 – The vocabulary. Four numbers, one formula. Don’t rush this slide. All examples today use WBTC on Aave V3. LTV: how much you borrowed relative to your collateral. 33% means $20k debt on $60k of BTC. You choose it, and it moves with the BTC price. Max LTV: how far the app lets you go when you open the loan. It is a ceiling, not a recommendation. For WBTC on Aave V3 it is 73%. Liquidation threshold: the LTV at which the protocol allows liquidators to repay part of your debt and take your collateral at a discount. For WBTC on Aave V3 it is 78%. Health factor: everything above in one number. Above 1 you are safe, below 1 you get liquidated. HF halves if BTC halves. Formula from my old workshop: HF = (collateral value × liquidation threshold) ÷ debt. Here: 60,000 × 0.78 ÷ 20,000 = 2.34. Next slide, the room does the math.
