Rates: Liberation Travel (Oct 2026). Midnight launch dates: The Block (Base), The Defiant (Ethereum).

0:23 – Morpho, the second protocol to know. Over $16B in deposits; builders include Coinbase and Société Générale. Morpho markets (Morpho Blue): each market is one collateral + one loan asset with fixed parameters. Nothing is re-lent, so a bad asset in another market can’t hurt you. – The cheap euro trick from Lupták’s article: WBTC/EURCV or cbBTC/EURCV at 86% LLTV, borrow rate around 2% (Oct 2026) – roughly half of Aave V3’s EURC rate at the time. – EURCV is a MiCA-regulated euro stablecoin from Société Générale-FORGE. Regulated also means the issuer can freeze. – Catches: little free liquidity (about 1.1M EURCV at the time), Ethereum mainnet gas only, and no close factor – a liquidator can repay 100% of your debt at once. So keep a higher health factor here than on Aave. Morpho Midnight: fixed-rate, fixed-term loans built into the protocol. Launched on Base on 21 July 2026 and on Ethereum on 8 September 2026 (USDC against WBTC or cbBTC). Good when you want to know your cost for the whole period. Tokenping (tokenping.xyz) sends reminders before Midnight loans mature. Quick demo if time allows: open the Morpho app and show a WBTC market’s LLTV, rate and liquidity.
