0:15 – The four practical routes for a BTC holder in 2026, with a $50k loan as the yardstick. The bars are one year of interest. 1. Firefish – the CeFi-feeling P2P route. KYC, euros on your bank account, a loan contract. ~50% LTV, 5–15% interest plus 1.5% origination. About $5,250 a year on $50k. Every renewal pays origination again. 2. Aave V3 – the classic DeFi route. No KYC. Borrow USDT ~3.3%, USDC ~4.0%, EURC ~4.5%, DAI ~4.9% (June 2026). ~$1,650 a year. 3. Morpho – isolated markets, one collateral and one loan asset each. Euro stablecoin markets around 2%. ~$1,000 a year. Thin liquidity, mainnet gas. 4. Aave v4 on Arc and Avalanche – the cheapest as of October 2026: EURC ~0.5% on Arc, USDT ~1.2% on Avalanche. ~$250–600 a year. Newest, so the least battle-tested. Over five years the gap is huge: ~$26k+ on Firefish vs ~$1,250–3,000 on Aave v4 (scaled from Lupták’s euro estimates). All rates float. Show the live rates in the demo instead of trusting this slide.
