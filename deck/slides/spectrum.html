0:38 – Section 06. Everything so far was over-collateralized: you lock more than you borrow. That works only for people who already have assets. The rest of the world borrows on reputation – a salary, a credit history, a relationship with a bank. The spectrum, left to right: – Secured, variable: Aave, Morpho, Firefish. – Peer-to-peer fixed terms: PWN, Morpho Midnight. Still collateralized, but negotiated. – Credit lines: Maple, Wildcat. Undercollateralized credit for institutions and market makers (Wildcat lines have gone to firms like Wintermute), underwritten off-chain, usually KYC’d and legally wrapped. – Reputation-based: 3Jane (Paradigm-backed, mixes onchain and offchain data; defaults hit your credit score and go to collections), and my project Unlloo (crowd lenders + reputation score). – Flash loans: zero collateral, but only inside one transaction. Why it’s hard: no collateral means the lender needs recourse or reputation. Wallets are cheap to create (sybils), so scores need history, identity attestations and skin in the game. Ask: “Would you lend $500 to a wallet you’ve never seen?
