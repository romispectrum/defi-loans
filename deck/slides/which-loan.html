0:57 – The cheat sheet. Leave this slide up while people ask questions or take photos. Walk it top to bottom: – Need fiat on a bank account with a contract and you’re fine with KYC? Firefish. – Privacy, flexibility, no end date? Aave (V3 for maturity and liquidity, v4 for the lowest rates). – Cheapest euros and you accept mainnet gas and thin liquidity? Morpho EURCV markets. – You want certainty about cost and date? Morpho Midnight or PWN. – No collateral at all? Unlloo, if your onchain reputation is good enough. – Just want leverage on BTC? A loan loop works, but perps (small, isolated margin, with a stop-loss) are usually cleaner. Above 10× leverage is basically a donation to the exchange. – Want the other side? Supply stablecoins, or lend on Unlloo at your own rate.
